# Spanish I Build Guide

Status: approved source-of-truth rules for future Spanish I curriculum work.

## Course Spine

- Homeschool Spanish Academy High School Level 1 is the spine for Spanish I.
- Supplemental materials follow the HSA sequence instead of forming a parallel course.
- Duolingo is supplemental practice only.
- The proficiency target is ACTFL Novice High, moving toward emerging Intermediate Low.
- Build and review one module at a time before moving on to the next module.

## Learners

- Materials are learner-focused first.
- Victor and Spencer receive the same academic level.
- Academic level is determined by ability, not age.
- Partner conversations are written for Victor and Spencer to practice together.
- Partner work should include role switching.
- Partner work should progress from modeled, to scaffolded, to unscripted.

## Spanish Usage

- Use idiomatic, broadly understood native Spanish.
- Label meaningful regional variation when it matters for learner understanding or real-world usage.

## Printable Materials

- Printable packet pages are designed for US Letter.
- Printable materials should feel professional and appropriate for language learning.
- Use meaningful diagrams and graphics, not decoration.
- Minimize reliance on color so pages remain useful when printed in grayscale.
- Include exactly one Parent / Instructor / Facilitator reference page at the end of each module.

## Website And Audio

- All audio lives on companion webpages, not in PDFs.
- Printable pages use Listen on Website markers where learners should use website audio.
- Longer readings receive Kindle Colorsoft-friendly versions.
