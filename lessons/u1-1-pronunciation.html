Step 2

High-value patterns from Lesson 1

Every practice word has its own audio target. Listen once, repeat once, then read the row without audio.

ñ

One sound, as in señor.

  • niño
  • español
  • mañana
  • señor
j / ge / gi

A breathy sound in many native varieties.

  • José
  • gente
  • gigante
silent h

The h is not pronounced.

  • hola
  • hermano
  • ahora
ll / y

Often similar in many regions.

  • yo
  • llamo
  • familia
r / rr

Tap r; trill rr as it develops.

  • pero
  • perro
  • gracias
qu

A k sound before e or i.

  • que
  • quiero
  • quince
gue / gui

Keep a hard g sound.

  • Miguel
  • guitarra
  • guerra
ce / ci / z

Latin America: s; much of Spain: th.

  • gracias
  • cinco
  • zapato

Regional Variation

Different native patterns can both be correct.

z, ce, ci

Latin America commonly uses an s sound. Much of Spain uses a th sound, as in English think.

ll and y

Many regions pronounce these very similarly. Some regions use a stronger zh or sh quality.

j, ge, gi

The breathy sound can be softer or stronger depending on the speaker and region.

Step 3

Victor and Spencer partner ladder

Switch roles after each round. Move from repeat-after-audio to independent speaking.

Modeled

Both repeat

Listen to each item, then both students repeat: a, e, i, o, u, niño, hola, gracias, quiero, familia.

Scaffolded

Read and identify

Victor reads one word. Spencer names the focus pattern. Then switch roles.

niño · José · hola · llamo · perro · que · guitarra · cinco

Unscripted

Choose and check

Each student chooses one family word, one greeting, one name or place, and one tricky word.

Step 4

Dos hermanos, un día ocupado

Read with support. The Spanish stretches listening and recognition; the written answers stay close to phrases students can retrieve from the passage.

Hola. Somos dos hermanos y vivimos en Washington.Hello. We are two brothers, and we live in Washington.

Nos llamamos Victor y Spencer. Estudiamos en casa y también jugamos al hockey.Our names are Victor and Spencer. We study at home, and we also play hockey.

Hoy tenemos un día ocupado. Por la mañana estudiamos matemáticas y español.Today we have a busy day. In the morning, we study math and Spanish.

Por la tarde vamos a la pista de hielo. Victor lleva el número trece y Spencer practica con su equipo.In the afternoon, we go to the ice rink. Victor wears number thirteen, and Spencer practices with his team.

Antes de salir, nuestra mamá pregunta: “¿Tienen todo?”Before leaving, our mom asks, “Do you have everything?”

Victor dice: “Sí. Tengo mis patines.” Spencer mira su bolsa y dice: “Un momento… ¿dónde están mis guantes?”Victor says, “Yes. I have my skates.” Spencer looks in his bag and says, “One moment… where are my gloves?”

Después de buscar un poco, los encuentra debajo de una silla.After looking for a little while, he finds them under a chair.

“¡Ahora sí!”, dice Spencer. Y todos salen de casa.“Now I’m ready!” says Spencer. And everyone leaves the house.
