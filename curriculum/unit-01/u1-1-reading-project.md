# U1.1 Interactive Reading And Identity Project

Status: approved curriculum content for website implementation.

Source context: persisted from Spanish Curriculum Planning after review of reading difficulty, comprehension question type, hockey equipment interaction, and mini-project design.

## Reading Difficulty Judgment

The reading is slightly above Victor and Spencer's current productive Spanish, but appropriate as supported comprehensible input.

Students do not need to independently produce forms such as `vivimos`, `estudiamos`, `tenemos`, `encuentra`, or `salen` yet. They can encounter those forms in a short, contextual story with familiar subject matter, English support, audio, and repeated cognates.

Design rule:

> Recognition can stretch above current production level; written responses should use structures students can reasonably retrieve and reproduce from the text.

## Mini Reading

### Dos hermanos, un día ocupado

English title: Two brothers, one busy day

Audio requirements:

- Full passage, natural learner-friendly pace.
- Full passage, slightly slower practice pace.
- Sentence-by-sentence playback.
- Optional sentence highlighting during playback.

Passage:

> **Hola. Somos dos hermanos y vivimos en Washington.**  
> Hello. We are two brothers, and we live in Washington.
>
> **Nos llamamos Victor y Spencer. Estudiamos en casa y también jugamos al hockey.**  
> Our names are Victor and Spencer. We study at home, and we also play hockey.
>
> **Hoy tenemos un día ocupado. Por la mañana estudiamos matemáticas y español.**  
> Today we have a busy day. In the morning, we study math and Spanish.
>
> **Por la tarde vamos a la pista de hielo. Victor lleva el número trece y Spencer practica con su equipo.**  
> In the afternoon, we go to the ice rink. Victor wears number thirteen, and Spencer practices with his team.
>
> **Antes de salir, nuestra mamá pregunta: "¿Tienen todo?"**  
> Before leaving, our mom asks, "Do you have everything?"
>
> **Victor dice: "Sí. Tengo mis patines." Spencer mira su bolsa y dice: "Un momento... ¿dónde están mis guantes?"**  
> Victor says, "Yes. I have my skates." Spencer looks in his bag and says, "One moment... where are my gloves?"
>
> **Después de buscar un poco, los encuentra debajo de una silla.**  
> After looking for a little while, he finds them under a chair.
>
> **"¡Ahora sí!", dice Spencer. Y todos salen de casa.**  
> "Now I'm ready!" says Spencer. And everyone leaves the house.

## Comprehension Questions

Use exactly three multiple-choice questions and two short-answer questions.

### 1. Multiple Choice

**¿Adónde van Victor y Spencer por la tarde?**  
Where do Victor and Spencer go in the afternoon?

- A. A la biblioteca.
- B. A la pista de hielo. Correct.
- C. Al supermercado.
- D. Al parque.

Audio required: Spanish question and each Spanish answer choice.

### 2. Multiple Choice

**¿Qué no encuentra Spencer al principio?**  
What can Spencer not find at first?

- A. Sus patines.
- B. Su casco.
- C. Sus guantes. Correct.
- D. Su botella de agua.

Audio required: Spanish question and each Spanish answer choice.

### 3. Multiple Choice

**¿Dónde están los guantes?**  
Where are the gloves?

- A. Dentro de la bolsa.
- B. Debajo de una silla. Correct.
- C. En el carro.
- D. Cerca de la puerta.

Audio required: Spanish question and each Spanish answer choice.

### 4. Short Answer

**¿Dónde viven Victor y Spencer?**  
Where do Victor and Spencer live?

Student writes in Spanish.

Expected answer:

> **Viven en Washington.**  
> They live in Washington.

Rationale: the structure is available directly in the passage.

### 5. Short Answer

**¿Qué estudian por la mañana?**  
What do they study in the morning?

Student writes in Spanish.

Expected answer:

> **Estudian matemáticas y español.**  
> They study math and Spanish.

Rationale: the structure is available directly in the passage.

## Personalized Hockey Equipment Interaction

Question:

**¿Qué no quieres olvidar antes de una práctica?**  
What do you never want to forget before practice?

Students choose from:

- **el casco** — helmet
- **los guantes** — gloves
- **la botella de agua** — water bottle
- **la ropa térmica** — base layers
- **los patines** — skates
- **el corazón de hierro** — the iron heart / mental toughness

The interaction should dynamically display the correct sentence after selection and map it to pre-produced audio. Do not generate speech on demand.

### Sentence Variants

Helmet:

> **Oye, bro, ¿trajiste tu casco hoy?**  
> Hey bro, did you bring your helmet today?

Gloves:

> **Oye, bro, ¿trajiste tus guantes hoy?**  
> Hey bro, did you bring your gloves today?

Water bottle:

> **Oye, bro, ¿trajiste tu botella de agua hoy?**  
> Hey bro, did you bring your water bottle today?

Base layers:

> **Oye, bro, ¿trajiste tu ropa térmica hoy?**  
> Hey bro, did you bring your base layers today?

Skates:

> **Oye, bro, ¿trajiste tus patines hoy?**  
> Hey bro, did you bring your skates today?

Iron heart:

> **Oye, bro, ¿trajiste hoy tu corazón de hierro?**  
> Hey bro, did you bring your iron heart today?

Natural sports-language follow-up:

> **¿Estás listo para competir?**  
> Are you ready to compete?

### Iron Heart Meaning

Use `el corazón de hierro` as a named family/team metaphor, not as a literal equipment item.

Explain once:

> **El corazón de hierro significa tener fortaleza mental, resiliencia y estar listo para competir.**  
> The iron heart means having mental toughness, resilience, and being ready to compete.

Natural Spanish concepts behind the metaphor:

- **fortaleza mental** — mental toughness
- **resiliencia** — resilience
- **mentalidad competitiva** — competitive mindset
- **estar listo para competir** — to be ready to compete

### Interaction Requirements

For each selectable item:

1. Show the selected item.
2. Display the correct Spanish sentence.
3. Display the English translation.
4. Use the matching pre-produced audio.
5. Offer a replay control.

## Reading Audio Standard

Target natural human/native-sounding voice. Avoid robotic or exaggerated learner audio.

Required audio:

- Full passage, natural learner-friendly pace.
- Full passage, slower practice pace.
- Sentence-by-sentence playback.
- All five comprehension question prompts.
- All Spanish multiple-choice options.
- All six personalized "Oye, bro..." variants.
- Natural sports-language follow-up for the iron-heart choice.

Use one consistent primary speaker for narration. Dialogue may use a second voice if practical, but this is an enhancement, not a requirement.

## Mini Project: Mi tarjeta: Así soy yo

This should be an online fill-in creation tool, not a worksheet.

The finished project should be Spanish only. Do not show English translations inside the final card. Students are expected to use prior lessons, dictionaries, or lookup tools to complete it.
